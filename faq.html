Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ordering, delivery and our products.

How far in advance should I order a custom cake? +
For standard birthday cakes we recommend at least 2–3 days’ notice. For wedding cakes and large or highly detailed designs, please give us 1–2 weeks (or more for peak seasons). Same-day or next-day orders may be possible depending on our schedule — just call us to check.
Do you deliver? +
Yes! We deliver within Siaya Town and nearby areas. Delivery fees depend on distance. You can save your preferred delivery address when you place an order or in your account for faster checkout next time.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept M-Pesa and Visa/Mastercard. You can pay online during checkout or arrange payment when you collect / on delivery.
Can I bring my own design or photo? +
Absolutely! On the Custom Order page you can upload an inspiration photo and describe exactly what you want. Our baker will work with you to create something as close as possible to your vision.
Do you cater for dietary requirements (eg. egg-free, nut-free)? +
We can often accommodate special requests such as egg-free or reduced sugar. Please mention any allergies or dietary needs when ordering so we can advise and prepare safely. Note that our kitchen handles common allergens.
What are your opening hours? +
We are generally open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am – 6:00 pm. Sunday and public holiday hours may vary — please call or WhatsApp 0713 773 913 to confirm.
Can I collect my order from the shop? +
Yes, collection from our shop in Siaya Town is always welcome and is free of charge. We’ll confirm the ready time when you place your order.
How do I leave a review? +
We’d love to hear from you! Visit the Reviews page, rate your experience, write a short comment and even upload photos of your cake. Your feedback helps us improve and helps other customers.
