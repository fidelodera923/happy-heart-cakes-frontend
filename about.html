About Happy Heart Cakes

Baking happiness into every creation, right here in Siaya Town.

Our Story

Happy Heart Cakes started with a simple belief: every celebration deserves a cake made with love. What began as a passion for baking has grown into a beloved bakery in Siaya Town, known for delicious cakes, friendly service, and creations that bring smiles.

We specialise in celebration cakes for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, graduations and more. At the same time, our shop counter is always stocked with fresh buns, small cakes, sweets and drinks for everyday enjoyment.

Whether you need a simple loaf cake for tea or a stunning multi-tier wedding centrepiece, we put the same care and quality into every order.
